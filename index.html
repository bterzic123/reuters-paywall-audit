What this is

We opened your paywall the way a reader does, then checked it against the market.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and this year we published State of Paywalls 2026: 78,000+ paywalls, what they look like and what wins in A/B tests. We put your app paywall next to it: the sheet that slides up over an article, its two plans, and what happens when someone swipes it away.

What follows is four ideas, each tied to a number from the report and each built into a clickable rebuild of your own sheet. Benchmarks are for all categories in Europe; the report has no News category.

We did not redesign your app

The prototype is your sheet: black over the dimmed article, your green ticks, your orange outline, your white Subscribe button. Only the offer around it changes. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes first, because that is what wins most often in the report. Each one opens the screen in the prototype.

01

Make Yearly the default, with a free week on it

Today Monthly is pre-selected while the BEST VALUE badge sits on Yearly, and there is no trial. €45 a year is never shown as €3.75 a month. Test Yearly selected, a 7-day trial on it, and the monthly price under it.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Annual pre-selected with the trial on it gave the biggest ARPU lifts. 7-day trials win on annual. See the screen ARPU +25–40%Observed in winning
A/B tests
02

Catch the swipe-down with one more offer

Swipe the sheet away and the reader is back on the article, with nothing in between. A second sheet offering Yearly at €3.75 a month gives that moment one more chance, using a price you already sell.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 4% of apps show a paywall after close. It converts at 0.88× the main one. See the screen ARPU +10–15%Based on
Adapty data
03

Show how the free week works

A trial only helps if readers believe they won’t be charged by surprise. One line under the plans covers it: today full access, a reminder on day 5, €45 on day 7.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 (Europe) · 55% of trials are cancelled within 24 hours. See the screen ARPU +12–36%Observed in winning
A/B tests
04

Put your 4.8 rating on the sheet

The headline says “globally trusted”. Your App Store listing proves it: 4.8 stars from 8.9K ratings in the US. Today the sheet never mentions it.
